FAQ

Not in here? Mail info@throttle.health or ask on r/ThrottleHealth.

I don’t lift. Is this for me?

Yes. Readiness, sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, stress and workouts work the same for everyone. The effort part only changes things for sessions where heart rate misses the load, like strength or HIIT. If you run, walk, cycle or don’t train at all, you get the full app.

Is it good for sleep only?

Yes. You get a sleep score out of 100 with every part shown, sleep debt over 14 days, a sleep need you set yourself and bedtime advice, all free. Want to know what goes with your good and bad nights? The journal in Pro shows that.

Does it replace my running app?

No. Throttle doesn’t do training plans or live tracking. It reads your runs from Apple Health and shows how your body handled them and how much today can take. Pro adds splits, running dynamics next to your usual range and pace zones.

What’s free?

Readiness, Target load, Sleep score and Recovery with every part explained. Sleep, sleep debt, sleep need and bedtime advice. HRV, resting heart rate, vitals, stress, steps and energy. Your workouts with heart rate, zones, effort, weather and the route map. The last 7 days in the calendar, your own Today screen, the Watch app and one Readiness widget. No ads, no time limit.

What does Pro add?

All your history and trends, training load and status, intensity minutes, VO2 max, fitness age and race times, the full running stats, records, heart rate recovery, the workout replay, the journal, the summary on Today with a morning and evening report, every widget, Watch complications, and Siri and Shortcuts. The full list is on the Free and Pro page.

How does the free trial work?

Pro starts with 14 days free. Cancel before that and you pay nothing. Either way you keep the free version.

What’s the launch price?

For the first 3 months after launch, the yearly plan is €14.99 instead of €19.99. Get in then and that price stays yours as long as you keep renewing.

Why not just use Apple’s Readiness?

Apple’s score only runs on Series 12 and Ultra 4. Throttle works from Series 6, shows what moved every score and by how much, and counts your effort for strength work. You also get Target load and sleep debt.

Does it work with my Watch?

You need an iPhone on iOS 27 and an Apple Watch Series 6 or newer, SE (2nd gen) or newer, or any Ultra. Wear it to bed for the sleep and recovery numbers.

When do I get my first scores?

Worn your Watch before? Throttle reads your history from Apple Health, so your scores are there on day one. New to Apple Watch? Recovery needs about 10 nights, Readiness about 2 weeks of training data.

Is there AI in it?

Only the short summary at the top of Today, part of Pro, made by Apple’s model on your iPhone itself, on iPhones with Apple Intelligence. Nothing leaves your phone. The scores themselves are just math.

Do you sell my data?

No. I never see your data. There is no account, and Throttle calculates everything on your iPhone from Apple Health. Maps and weather go through Apple, with an approximate location and without your health data.

Is this a medical app?

No. It’s for training and recovery. It doesn’t diagnose anything. If something feels off with your health, see a doctor.

Android?

No. Throttle is built on Apple Health and Apple Watch, and I’m one guy.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Apple handles the payments. You keep the free version.

I tested the beta. What now?

Install Throttle from the App Store. You get 3 months of Pro for free. I’ll post on r/ThrottleHealth how to get your code.
