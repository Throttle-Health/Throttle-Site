What's in Throttle.

The home page shows Readiness and how effort counts. Here's the rest, with what's free and what's in Pro.

Sleep

A sleep score you can check.

Out of 100, and you see every part of it. So when you wake up wrecked with a decent score, you can see which part is off.

All free. You don't have to train at all to use it.

Running

For runners.

Every run comes in with heart rate, zones, weather and the route map, free. Pro lets you replay the run with your heart rate along the way, and shows power, cadence, stride and ground contact next to your own usual range. It doesn't do training plans. It tells you how your body is handling yours.

Goals

PRO

Set a goal. See how often you hit it.

Steps, running distance, intensity minutes, workouts, active energy, flights climbed, daylight or food. Per day, week, month or year. Next to the field you see your own average, so you pick a number that fits you.

Every goal gets its own colour, so you can tell them apart at a glance. They also live on your Home Screen as a widget and on your Watch. You get a message when you reach one, never one that says you're behind.

PRO

Tag your day.

Caffeine, alcohol, a late meal, sauna, travel. Tap what applies, or let Throttle tag things like a long training by itself. After enough nights, Insights puts your average Recovery after a tag next to your other nights. It shows what goes together. It doesn't claim what causes what.
